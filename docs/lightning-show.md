## The Physics

Light in mica-loaded mineral oil doesn't travel in straight lines. It **scatters**.

Each mica particle is a flat reflective disc (~20-100μm). When LED light hits it:
- Some light reflects off the face (specular flash)
- Some light passes through and hits the next particle
- Some light scatters sideways into the oil

The result is **volumetric branching light** — photons cascading through a 3D scattering medium. The branching pattern depends on:
- Mica density (particles per mL)
- Mica size (larger = more specular, smaller = more diffuse)
- Oil flow (pump + thermal convection shifts the particle field)
- LED position (bottom-up vs side vs top)
- LED color (shorter wavelengths scatter more — blue scatters wider than red)

**This IS a neural network made of light.** Each mica particle is a node. Each scatter event is a connection. The topology changes with oil flow.

## Mica Density Tuning

The density of mica particles controls the lightning character:

| Density | Visual | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Very low (0.1g/L) | Long straight beams, few branches | Laser-like, precise, cold |
| Low (0.5g/L) | Medium branches, some scatter | Clean, structured |
| Medium (1.0g/L) | Rich branching, visible paths | **Sweet spot — lightning-like** |
| High (2.0g/L) | Dense fog, diffuse glow | Nebula-like, dreamy |
| Very high (5.0g/L) | Opaque, no individual paths | Lava lamp (too dense) |

**Recommended: 1.0g/L** — enough particles for visible branching, sparse enough to see individual scatter paths. The lightning effect requires space between particles.

Each personality could tune density slightly:
- Solin (⚡): 1.2g/L — dense, electric, more branches
- Phex (🔱): 0.8g/L — cleaner, more structured paths
- Lux (🔆): 0.5g/L — long beams, cathedral-like

## LED Hardware for Lightning Effect

The key to good lightning is **individual LED addressability + fast switching.**

WS2812B at 800kHz data rate:
- 60 LEDs update in 2.25ms (fast enough for lightning)
- Individual brightness per LED (0-255)
- Individual color per LED
- One data pin from Pi 4B GPIO

For the lightning effect, we need LEDs **distributed throughout the oil volume**, not just in a strip on the wall:

```
Oil Chamber LED Layout:
┌──────────────────────────┐
│  ○  ○  ○  ○  ○  ← top ring (10 LEDs, near surface)
│                          │
│     ○    ○    ○  ← middle ring (6 LEDs, Pi 5 level)
│  ○     ○     ○          │
│                          │
│  ○  ○  ○  ○  ○  ← bottom ring (10 LEDs, near pump)
│        ↑                 │
│      pump                │
└──────────────────────────┘

Plus: 4 LEDs on vertical posts (corner pillars of the baffle frame)
  These create the vertical lightning bolts (bottom to top paths)

Total: 30 LEDs inside oil + 30 LEDs on sphere interior wall
  = 60 LEDs total, all on one data line
```

The 30 LEDs inside the oil are on waterproof (IP68 silicone-sleeved) WS2812B strips, mounted to the baffle frame. The 30 on the sphere wall create the "surface glow" background.

**The lightning comes from the oil-submerged LEDs.** The wall LEDs provide ambient color. The oil LEDs provide the branches.
